How a session actually gets confirmed

The whole point is that a code can't be forwarded to someone across town. Here's the sequence a student goes through every time.

01

Sign in fresh

Every session starts with a real sign-in — no saved sessions carried over from last lecture.

02

Go offline

Wi-Fi and mobile data off. After a 10-second hold, the app confirms you're disconnected.

03

Face the projector

The camera reads the lecturer's rotating code and checks its timestamp against your device clock.

04

Three confirms

Once three fresh codes land within five seconds of real time, attendance is locked in locally.
